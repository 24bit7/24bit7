**The "Source test ready" line no longer turns up in the middle of a playlist build.**

Test My Sources runs in the background, and when it finished during a build, its "Source test ready" line landed among the build's own lines, as if it were part of the playlist. It now waits until the build has finished and appears just after its Done line. With no build running, it shows straight away as before.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
