**Ask Claude about a build.** With Console Query switched on, click into the console, press Query and ask why a playlist came out the way it did. Claude reads the console, your settings and 24bit7's own code, then explains what happened and suggests settings to change.

- **Console Query.** Off by default: Settings > Other > Enable Console Query. It uses more Anthropic credit than the AI Moderator, a few pence for the first question and much less for follow-ups. Your keys and passwords are never sent.
- **Copy and Clear.** Click into the console to copy what you've highlighted (or all of it), or to clear it.
- **Drift and Non-stop on the Play tab.** Both sit in More Options beside the AI Moderator and change Settings > Playlist for every Play option at once.
- **Band names matched both ways.** "The Jimi Hendrix Experience" now finds a library tagged "Jimi Hendrix", and the reverse. "At most 3 per artist" counts them as one artist.
- **Playlists and smartlists set apart.** "Use global playlist settings" has a row for each, so a random-album smartlist can keep its own order.
- **Now Playing refreshes on click.**
- **Rounded buttons and tabs.**

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
