**Songs a source names with a version after a dash, like "Don't Stop Me Now - Remastered 2011", now find your copy.**

A version in brackets was always ignored when matching against your library, but one after a dash wasn't, so Radio and every other playlist could call a song you own "not in library". The last part after a dash is now dropped when it's a version (Remastered, Live, Mix, Edit and the like), and a title with a dash that belongs to the song is still found.

- **Discover's Suggested by** shows the sources that named a track for Radio, Play & Embellish and Blend, as it does for the other playlists, instead of "Radio" or "Embellish". Rows already logged keep what they had.
- **Discover's Seed** says "Radio: Classic Rock / Rock" or "Playlist: Happy Go Lucky" where it used to say "? - Classic Rock / Rock".

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
