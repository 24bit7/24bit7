**Albums on the Play tab: play one, or embellish it with the artist's hits or with artists like them, and a one-hit wonder now gives you its hit rather than the rest of its songs.**

The JRiver Playlists tab is now Playlists & Albums. Type part of a name into its box to find an album or a playlist, or an artist's name for their albums; each row says which it is. Play plays an album in order to start. Embellish, as Play & Embellish is now called, gives an album two choices of its own: With Their Hits shuffles the artist's widely played songs from their other albums through it, and And Their Hits plays the album, then those songs, biggest first. The amounts work on an album too. An album, or a playlist with one artist, used to have nothing to embellish it with; now the circle is the artist and two of the five artists most like them, picked at random each time.

Use Adaptive Selections is a new tick in Playlist Selection, on to start. It works out how many of an artist's songs are widely played, from Last.fm's play counts, and takes no more than that: 4 Non Blondes give you What's Up?, Radiohead still give you plenty.

- Play and Embellish are outlined in the accent colour, like Clear Playing Now.
- Play has no Non-stop of its own any more: an album ends with its last track. For more, open More Options and set its Non-stop switch. Before, Play carried on as the standard Artist Matching playlist's Non-stop said.
- With nothing to add, Embellish now plays the album or playlist as it is, rather than nothing.
- German: Settings > Playlists & Alben's Skip recent column is Kürzlich gespielt, so Als Vorgabe setzen fits beside it.
- The manual says what "rougher results" means for small local AI models.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
