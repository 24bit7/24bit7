**Multiroom: your main system plays what your Sonos plays, in step, with the Stop and Skip Track buttons on the Now Playing row for any zone.**

**Multiroom** is experimental, and so far tested on one Sonos One and a Chord Hugo over ASIO. Tick it on under Settings > Other > Multiroom, pick the zone that plays to the Sonos (the leader) and the zone whose DAC follows, and the Play tab's Output list gains Multiroom. A build sent with it goes to the Sonos as usual, and 24bit7 plays the same music on the other DAC, placed on the Sonos's own clock to within a few milliseconds, at the file's own sample rate and with your DSP, from a second JRiver DLNA server you set up once. Track changes stay gapless, jumps are followed, a pause anywhere stops both rooms and play starts the track from the top in both. The Speakers join a track already playing, and leave at the end of the track when Output moves away.

- **Stop** and **Skip Track** on the Now Playing row, for whichever zone it shows. Stop, in amber, stops the zone (both rooms in Multiroom); once stopped it reads **Clear Playing Now**, in red, and one click empties the list. Skip Track counts for the Skipped Tracks filter, as a voice skip does.
- **Zone** gains a Multiroom entry that shows the leader's track, picked by itself when the Speakers start following.
- **Multiroom and Single Room by voice**: "ask needle drop multiroom" and the main system joins what the Sonos is playing; "single room" keeps the music in the room of the Echo that heard it. The skill needs its new interaction model and code pasted in, as the manual's Upgrading section says.
- Settings > Other > Multiroom finds the speaker, the DAC and the DLNA server, has **Check Clock** to say whether a speaker's clock can be followed, and shows the three timing numbers from the profiles, with **Nudge** to move them by ear.
- **Calibrate** measures those numbers for your own kit from one phone recording of about three minutes, made where you listen: Play Test Sound, record the clicks in both rooms, Load Recording, and the result comes back with how well two fresh starts agree. Nothing changes until you press Use These Numbers, and Copy a Line for the Forum gives a one-liner so other kit's numbers can become profiles.

Multiroom needs a Sonos today; other network speakers come once Check Clock and a calibration say they can be followed. Nothing changes for anyone who leaves it off.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
