**Filters have a page of their own again, with a Main Filter every playlist shares, and the original plays when you own a song more than once.**

Under Settings > Filters, tick **In Main Filter** on a filter and every playlist using the Main Filter goes by it, which is every playlist to start. A config that needs something else, say a kitchen playlist that can play anything, sets **Use Main Filter** to No and ticks its own. Your filters come across as they were: anything you had on Every playlist, or ticked on every playlist, makes up the Main Filter. When you own a song more than once, the copy that plays is now the title asked for, then one not on an album named live, then the earliest, so the studio version beats a later live album, best-of or remix.

- **Discover > Missing Artists** says which album to buy first: "Most are on Satellite (6 of 7), then Testify (1)", from the artist's albums on Deezer, counting each song for the album it first came out on.
- **Radio's stations** that split have a small box at their left. Click the box, or anywhere left of the name, to open them in place; a near miss no longer plays the station.
- **Multiroom** carries on if the main system's DAC won't open: the console says so once, the Sonos plays on, and the main system joins in when the DAC is back.
- **Alexa** checks her own connection and says in the console if the address she calls stops answering. "Which artist?" can be answered with just the name, and Settings > Voice Commands says when your skill is older than this version's.
- After 24bit7 has had to start JRiver again, the console says what Windows recorded about the crash. The update window speaks German when 24bit7 does.

**If you use the Alexa skill:** paste the new `alexa/interaction_model.json` into the skill's JSON Editor and `alexa/lambda_function.py` into its code, leave your `skill_settings.py` as it is, then Build and Deploy. See the manual's Upgrading section.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
