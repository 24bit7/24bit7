**Hidden tracks are found rather than guessed: a long album closer plays, and 24bit7 moves on where the silence before its hidden track starts.**

Hidden Tracks used to leave out every album closer longer than six minutes, which threw away real songs: System Of A Down's Aerials is under four minutes with its hidden track making six. It's now a choice of three, starting on **Listen for Them**: 24bit7 checks an album's last track over five minutes for a gap with sound after it, ten seconds of quiet or a short complete silence late in the track. If it finds one, the song plays and 24bit7 moves on where the silence starts; the console says "Aerials by System Of A Down: plays to 3:53, its hidden track left out." If not, the track plays in full. Each file is checked once, in the background after the playlist is sent, and remembered. Your files are only read, never changed. On two hundred 1990s albums it found every hidden track and cut nothing it shouldn't, though a song with a long silence before its final section could lose that section. A file Windows can't read (DSD, APE or WavPack) is left out, with a line saying why. **Over Minutes** keeps the old rule for anyone who prefers it, and **Off** plays closers in full. Albums and playlists you ask for by name still play in full.

- **Long Tracks**, a new ready-made filter, off to start: turn it on under Settings > Filters to keep tracks of fifteen minutes or more out of every playlist.
- **Multiroom joins in step.** When your main system joins something the Sonos is already playing, both rooms now start the track again together, or go on to the next one past halfway, rather than joining part way through, which could leave a slight echo.
- **Discover:** Refresh on Missing Artists and Gaps in Your Artists reads your library again, so what you've bought since drops off, and a song you own now counts as matched in Gaps. The chosen row is filled with the accent colour, so it stands out.
- **The console tidies itself:** lines that aren't part of a build, such as Stop and Skip Track, clear after ninety seconds while the window is showing and the mouse isn't over it.
- Stop turns into Clear Playing Now as soon as JRiver has stopped, rather than up to ten seconds later.
- Edit Filters opens at the size its filter needs rather than nearly filling the screen.
- Play and Embellish on the Playlists & Albums tab are one line tall, and the Album or Playlist box beside them is wider.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
