**"Multiroom" in the Output list now means one thing, and an Output that isn't switched on says so.**

If you had a JRiver zone called Multiroom and the Multiroom feature switched off, picking that zone under Output sent the playlist to Same zone without a word. Now the name is your zone while the feature is off, and while the feature is on a zone of that name is left out of the Output and Zone lists so it shows once. If Output asks for Multiroom, YouTube or Tidal and that isn't switched on in Settings, the console says where the playlist went instead.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
