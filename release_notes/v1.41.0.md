**Copy an album or playlist to your phone without playing it, into artist and album folders, with a window that shows how it's going.**

On the Playlists & Albums tab, pick an album or playlist and **Copy to Phone** appears at the top right: it opens the same choices as Save Playlist's Copy Tracks To, without the name, and copies the tracks without playing anything or saving a JRiver playlist. Copied tracks now go into artist and album folders on the phone, even for one track, with the playlist's .m3u8 at the top of the folder you chose. A window shows the track being copied, how far it's got and roughly how long is left, with **Stop**; copy again and it picks up where it left off.

- **Browse**, beside Folder, opens the phone's folders, for music kept somewhere other than Music (Music/Music, say). It won't take the phone's top level, so Clear First can never empty the whole phone.
- A song another playlist copied to the same phone still lists stays when it leaves this one.
- A playlist copied by 1.40.0 into a folder of its own moves into the new folders the next time it's copied.
- Play's arrow on Playlists & Albums plays at once when you pick Play Shuffled or Play Ordered, as Embellish's does.
- Stacking artists on Search says one short line each: "Seether added to the stack, now 11 artists."

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
