**AI from OpenAI, Google Gemini or your own PC.** Settings > Keys > AI now has a Provider choice: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or Ollama, which runs a model on your own computer for free. Pick one and add its key (or, for Ollama, choose a model), and every AI feature uses it: AI Playlist, Steer, the AI Moderator, the AI as a source and the Queries. Only Anthropic has been fully tested so far. OpenAI, Gemini and Ollama should work, and reports on how they do are very welcome on the JRiver forum or GitHub. Console Query isn't available with Ollama.

- **Show Credits, by track or album.** Settings > Other > Show Credits lists the playing track's credits or every track's, alongside the album's, in the order you choose. Credits that Discogs marks for some tracks only, such as a drummer on tracks 2 to 4, now appear on those tracks.
- **Switch Profiles** in Keyboard Shortcuts: each press loads the next profile ticked under Enable Switch To. Pressed during a build, it waits until the build has finished.
- **Thanks** in the README and Settings > About, to datdude, Helmut and Denis.
- **Fixes:** Use AI and Run After Building could be forgotten after a restart until a setting was saved, so the AI could run with Use AI Off. Hidden control characters in tags no longer stop a library loading or Now Playing being read (found by Denis on a 187,000-track library). The AI cost note in Settings > Keys now wraps inside the window.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
