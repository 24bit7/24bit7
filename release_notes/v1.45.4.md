**A playlist sent to a paused zone starts straight away, and in Multiroom your main system only follows music from JRiver.**

If something else left the Sonos paused (an Alexa flash briefing that got stuck, say), a playlist from the Play tab used to be queued behind the paused track, and nothing started it. A paused zone now counts as free, just like a stopped one, so the playlist plays at once. A zone that's playing still finishes its track first.

In Multiroom, your main system used to follow whatever the Sonos was playing, even if it wasn't from JRiver, so pressing Play could put JRiver's track in one room and the news in the other. Now, when the Sonos plays something else, the main system waits and the console says so once. It joins in again when the Sonos is back on JRiver.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
