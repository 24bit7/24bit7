**Asking Alexa for radio before you have any stations now tells you how to make them.**

Until Radio has stations, "Alexa, ask needle drop for radio" now says: "You don't have any stations yet. When the Radio button is ready, choose Analyse Now to make your stations." It says the same whether Radio is still waiting for more of your plays or just hasn't been analysed yet. The skill itself hasn't changed, so there's nothing to paste into the Alexa console.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
