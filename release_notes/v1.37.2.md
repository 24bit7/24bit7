**When JRiver has stopped, 24bit7 now brings back its Media Server, without opening JRiver's window.**

1.37.1 started the whole of JRiver after a crash, so its window popped up. All 24bit7 needs is JRiver's Media Server, the part that keeps running when you close the window, and it's what JRemote and other PCs use too. So that's what 24bit7 starts now, just as JRiver's own Media Server shortcut does. Open JRiver as usual if you want the window.

- Two voice requests made while JRiver was starting could collide once it was ready, and one of them was lost. They now play one after the other.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
