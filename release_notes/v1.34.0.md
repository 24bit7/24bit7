**Discover shows the artists your sources keep naming that you don't own, and the songs missing from artists you do.**

Discover is now three tabs. **Sessions** is Discover as it was, one build at a time. **Missing Artists** reads the whole history and ranks the artists you don't own by how many of your artists point to them; select one to see whose lists it's on, the songs named, Label and your store buttons. **Gaps in Your Artists** lists the songs you don't have by artists you own.

- **Genre** narrows both to one of Radio's stations, counted by the artists of yours that point to each missing artist. It's greyed while Radio is resting.
- **Sound** narrows a tab to the artists sharing a Last.fm tag, such as nu metal, britpop or 80s. Different spellings count as one, and a tag that only restates the station is greyed. The first time either tab opens, 24bit7 asks Last.fm in the background for the tags of artists it doesn't know yet, and keeps them with Radio's.
- **Period** (all time, six months, twelve) and **Zone**, with who asked beside each zone. Genre, Period and Zone carry across the two tabs. Every build now keeps the zone it played in and who asked for it; earlier builds take theirs from the console's Log, which began on 5 October 2026.
- Neither Genre nor Sound reads your library's own Genre tags.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
