**Discover has a Statistics tab: the artists your sources keep naming that you don't own, the songs missing from artists you do, and how each source is doing.**

Discover is now two tabs. **Sessions** is Discover as it was, one build at a time. **Statistics** reads the whole history. **Missing Artists** ranks the artists you don't own by how many of your artists point to them; select one to see whose lists it's on, the songs named, Label and your store buttons. **Gaps in Your Artists** lists the songs you don't have by artists you own. **Sources** shows what each source named, how much of it is in your library, and what it found that no other source did.

- **Genre** narrows it to one of Radio's stations, counted by the artists of yours that point to each missing artist. It's greyed while Radio is resting.
- **Sound** narrows it to the artists sharing a Last.fm tag, such as nu metal, britpop or 80s. Different spellings count as one, and a tag that only restates the station is greyed. The first time Statistics opens, 24bit7 asks Last.fm in the background for the tags of artists it doesn't know yet, and keeps them with Radio's.
- **Period** (all time, six months, twelve) and **Zone**, with who asked beside each zone. Every build now keeps the zone it played in and who asked for it; earlier builds take theirs from the console's Log, which began on 5 October 2026.
- Neither Genre nor Sound reads your library's own Genre tags.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
