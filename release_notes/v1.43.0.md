**Copy playlists to your phone over WiFi with the new 24bit7 phone app, or to a folder on this PC, and see and tidy what's there with Manage Folder.**

The 24bit7 phone app is attached below as **24bit7-phone-v1.43.0.apk**. Put it on an Android phone and open it: it shows a four-digit code. In Save's Device list, **+ Add a Phone Over WiFi...** takes the code and a name and finds the phone on your home network, so copies go without the cable. Keep the app open while it copies. A phone you also plug in by USB stays one device, with one name, folder and limit.

- **This PC** is in the Device list too: a folder here, for Syncthing and the like.
- **Clear First keeps what the new playlist needs**, so only what's missing is copied.
- Without Clear First, the other playlists in the folder lose songs no longer there, and a playlist left empty is taken off.
- A playlist emptied in JRiver can be saved to the phone to take it off.
- **Open Folder**, beside Browse, opens the folder in Explorer, on this PC or a phone plugged in.
- **Manage Folder** shows the folder's playlists, then its artists and albums, with sizes against your limit. Tick and Delete: an album comes out of every playlist that lists it, and the amber line above Delete says what will go.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
