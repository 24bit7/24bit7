**Play & Embellish has settings of its own, and Play & Embellish, Radio and AI Playlist no longer build in silence.**

Settings > JRiver Playlists > Play & Embellish is now a table. One line, All amounts, sets **Shuffle**, **Deep Cuts** with its share, and the **Moderator** for every amount. Untick **Use the same settings for every amount** and each amount from +10% to Build It Out has a line of its own, a copy of that one until you change it. Deep Cuts top up what Embellish added, from the genre most of your playlist shares, and the Moderator checks only the new tracks against the playlist. The More Options switches still beat them while that row is open.

- **Shuffle is the default** wherever a JRiver playlist plays: Play & Embellish, and a playlist or smartlist asked for by voice on a fresh install. If Play Ordered was your choice, Embellish starts with Shuffle unticked, and voice rows you've saved keep what they say, so nothing changes at the upgrade.
- **No build waits in silence.** On a stopped zone Play & Embellish starts the playlist's own first track at once, Radio starts a pillar's song, and AI Playlist makes a quick first request for an opener that plays while the full list is found. The quick request is left out with Ollama, in Steer, and when music is already playing.
- **Radio and Play & Embellish show their working.** Each pillar and agreeing artist is a line in the console, each of its top tracks In library or Not in library, and every one is listed in Discover, where their sessions used to be empty.
- **Multiroom's running commentary** now shows in the Advanced console only. A Multiroom problem, or a note about something you can hear, shows in Simple too.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
