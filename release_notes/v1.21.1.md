**A maximised window opens maximised next time.** If you maximised 24bit7 and then quit it or restarted the PC, it opened again at a smaller size and had to be maximised by hand. It now remembers and opens the way you left it, including when you open it from the tray after Windows starts it there.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
