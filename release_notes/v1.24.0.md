**24bit7 now speaks German.** Choose Deutsch under Settings > Other > Language and, after a restart, the screens and the console are in German, with a German manual, 24bit7_Handbuch.pdf, beside the English one. Song, artist and album names stay as they are, and Alexa stays English. If you'd like 24bit7 in another language, ask on the JRiver forum or GitHub.

- **An empty saved playlist** now says when JRiver refused the tracks for want of a login, and how to set one up.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
