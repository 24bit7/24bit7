**Close Review puts the console back, and the Play tab is tidier.** A few small things around Review, the console and added playlists, each asked for after a while of use.

- **Close Review**: a button at the top right of Review, above Stop Preview. It drops the list and shows the console again, cleared, as it was before the first Review build.
- **The console greeting stays in one piece.** In Advanced mode, lines logged at start-up could land in the middle of it. They now wait until it clears.
- **Added playlists**: the grey line under the rows saying what the next build will do has gone. The console says it instead, once per change.
- **Most used playlists**: in the playlist picker, each most used playlist has an x at its right that takes it out of that list. The playlist itself, and any row using it, are untouched.
- **Naming things**: Save as Playlist, Save Profile, New Filter and Rename Filter ask for the name in a box drawn like the rest of 24bit7, in place of the plain Windows one.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
