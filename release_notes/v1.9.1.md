**AI for Similar Tracks.** AI can now be ticked as a source for Similar Tracks, as it already could for Similar Artists. Claude suggests songs like the seed track, judged on sound, mood, energy and era, and its picks blend and vote with Last.fm, ListenBrainz and YouTube like any other source. It's off by default and uses a little Anthropic credit per build; repeat seeds come from the cache for free.

- **Up to 4 sources can agree** on a track, now there can be four.
- **AI ticks are purple** in both source lists, like everything else that uses credit.
- **Drift's Custom Sources never include AI**, so Drift from your sources stays AI-free.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
