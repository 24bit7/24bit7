**Steadier playlists without more AI.** The AI Moderator now has levels, Drift can use its own sources, and bracketed song titles match your library however they're tagged.

- **AI Moderator levels.** Off, Relaxed, Balanced or Strict, on the Play tab and per device. Relaxed removes only clear clashes, at most a fifth of the tracks. Balanced removes anything that noticeably shifts the tone, energy or mood, at most two fifths. Strict keeps only tracks close to the seed, however many that leaves. Off by default; if you had it on, it's now Balanced.
- **Drift with Custom Sources.** Each Drift section can use its own sources and its own "must agree". For example: Last.fm for the first pass, then Deezer and ListenBrainz with 2 agreeing to fill the gaps. It stays the same as Settings > Sources unless you change it.
- **Vibe Playlist is now AI Playlist.** Its first suggestion is More tracks like the song that's playing. The AI's own picks are never moderated. Its Drift says plainly when it's using your sources rather than the AI, with an optional AI Moderator for those tracks.
- **Bracketed titles match both ways.** "Long Cool Woman (in a Black Dress)" now finds "Long Cool Woman in a Black Dress", and the reverse. Version tags such as (Remastered) are still ignored. On the development library, 4,196 of 4,390 bracketed titles would have missed when a source wrote them differently.
- **Nothing found still finishes the job.** Run After Building now runs after every build, matches or not, and added playlists play on their own when nothing matched.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
