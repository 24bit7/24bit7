**Seed: Playing Now.** Similar Artists and Similar Tracks can now start from your whole Playing Now list instead of the track that's playing. Open More Options and click Seed: Current Track to switch it to Playing Now (the button and the bars under the two buttons turn teal). A few artists or tracks are picked at random from the list, and each in turn brings its own share of similar ones, so no artist repeats and the results are usually broader than Current Track. Tracks already in Playing Now are left out. Settings > Playlist has new Playing Now columns for the figures, and Review Mode gets its own pair. Discover shows which Playing Now track each suggestion came from.

- **If All Else Fails.** When a build finds nothing (or nothing beyond the seed itself), 24bit7 now shuffles songs from your library in the seed's genre, at a similar tempo where BPM tags allow, instead of stopping. Voice commands hear why in the room first. On by default for each playlist type in Settings > Playlist.
- **Only library artists count.** A similar artist now only counts towards Number of artists once it gives a track from your library; one that doesn't is skipped and the next one down is read instead. This applies to Current Track too, so playlists are fuller and more consistent.
- **Cancel a build.** While a build runs, click into the console and press Cancel. It stops at its next step and says what, if anything, had already gone out.
- **More Options redesigned.** Seed, Play Mode / Review Mode, AI Moderator, Drift and Non-stop are now buttons: click to step through, right-click to step back.
- **Search by artist alone.** On the Search tab, Similar Artists now needs only the artist.
- **Tidier tabs:** a cleaner Settings > Playlist, softer search boxes, and Discover's buttons rounded, with A- and A+ for the text size.
- **Fixes:** the AI Moderator skipped a Drift round that found a single track, without saying so (found by datdude); Balanced and Strict now check it, and Relaxed says when it skips. With OpenAI, Google Gemini or Ollama chosen, notes and errors no longer talk about Anthropic credit (found by Denis).

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one. Your Review figures carry over unchanged.
