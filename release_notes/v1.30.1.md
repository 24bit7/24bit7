**Playlist Settings in smaller text, with A- and A+ to suit your screen, and a page that scrolls as one.**

The Playlist Settings tab is drawn at the size of its notes, so more of a playlist fits on the page, and two small buttons at the top right, **A-** and **A+**, set the text size of every Settings screen, kept in your .env. The left column (Your Playlist Buttons and Devices) and the page itself each scroll as one piece, header included, so nothing at the bottom is out of reach on a short window.

- **Consensus** and **Moderator** sit under the configs with the other whole-playlist sections, not inside the config box above Playlist Selection. A playlist with both an Artist Matching and a Track Matching config gets one of each, titled by kind.
- **Devices**, as Device Playlists is now called: click a row and its page opens on the right, no pop-up, with **Copy Settings From:** and a list of your playlists. Ticked, the phrase runs that playlist as it is, any kind, and you pick another to change it. Untick it and the device gets its own copy to change. Last heard has gone.
- **Review Mode uses the same settings** sits on its own line under Skip tracks on every standard playlist that reviews, Artist's Top Tracks included. Untick it and Review Mode's own lines open in an orange box.
- The **Preview** is half its old size and sits beside Name, its text following A- and A+. **Take a Tour** sits under Simple / Advanced instead of taking a row of its own. The Sources ticks are the same size as the rest.
- Square corners again: the rounded ones from 1.30.0 didn't draw cleanly on every screen.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
