**In Discover, each Radio track's Seed is now the pillar that led to it.**

The session list still says "Radio: Classic Rock / Rock", but each row now says where it came from: a pillar's own song shows that pillar, and an artist the pillars agreed on shows the first pillar that named it. Rows logged before this version keep the station name, since they didn't record a pillar.

- **Missing Artists** counts an artist Radio missed under the pillar that pointed to it.
- **Review's Found By** says who a Radio track was like, for example "Last.fm named Foghat as like Led Zeppelin".

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
