**Deep Cuts can now be switched on for an Echo Dot or speaker with settings of its own.** A device with its own settings builds from its own copy of them, so the Play tab's Deep Cuts button never reached it. Its Playlist tab under Settings now has the switch, Off, Spaced or Shuffled, beside Top Up with Deep Cuts by. Devices that copy Windows (Main) follow the Play tab's button, as before.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
