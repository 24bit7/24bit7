**You can now see a voice request being built, from across the room.** While a voice command builds a playlist, a small badge sits in the top right of the screen, on top of everything, JRiver's Theater View included. It shows what you asked for and the zone, stays up while Drift is still adding tracks, then says how many went in, or why none did, and fades. So a request Alexa misheard no longer leaves you waiting for nothing. It never takes focus and clicks go straight through it, so the remote carries on as normal.

- **Voice badge:** the dot moves while 24bit7 works, magenta while the AI is choosing. A command that comes to nothing shows what Alexa said. It follows your accent colour.
- **Filters:** if you keep a song as a stereo MP3 and a surround FLAC, and a filter leaves out the copy 24bit7 found, your other copies are tried, and the first that passes is used. A live recording doesn't count as a copy of the studio one.
- **Recently played** now counts any copy, so playing the MP3 yesterday keeps the FLAC out today too.
- **Only** finds an artist with or without the gaps in its name: "ACDC" finds AC/DC, "gusgus" finds Gus Gus.
- **Names with N:** "Guns and Roses" finds Guns N' Roses, and "Salt and Pepa" finds Salt-N-Pepa. This works everywhere 24bit7 matches names, not just Only.
- **Apostrophes:** a straight apostrophe and a curly one now count as the same, so an artist tagged both ways (Jane's Addiction, say) is one artist, not two.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
