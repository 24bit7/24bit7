**Voice playlists now let the playing song finish first, and Alexa has two new commands: "only" and "more artists like this".**

When you ask for a playlist by voice and the zone is already playing, the song carries on, what was queued after it is cleared, and your new playlist follows. To have it take over straight away as before, set **New Playlists** to Play now under Settings > Voice Commands. "Only chill Led Zeppelin" builds an AI Playlist from that one artist's tracks in your library, and nobody else gets in. "More artists like this" builds Similar Artists from what's playing.

- **No two in a row:** every playlist except Artist's Top Tracks is spread so the same artist never plays twice running, where that can be avoided.
- **AI Playlist** has its own **Most tracks per artist** setting (Settings > Playlist), three to start, so a mood the AI links to one band doesn't fill up with them.
- **In German,** Settings > About now lists the voice commands in German, and the listener's status and the New Key button read properly.
- **The Alexa skill has changed.** For the new commands, paste the new `alexa\interaction_model.json` and `alexa\lambda_function.py` into the Alexa developer console, as in Steps 4 and 5 of Setting up the Alexa skill, then Build and Deploy.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
