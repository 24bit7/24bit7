**An AI Playlist that comes back empty now tells you when your filters left the tracks out.** If the AI picked songs you own but your filters or the recently played check left them all out, the console used to say nothing it picked was in your library. It now says how many were found and what left them out, so you know to look at Settings > Filters or Settings > Playlist rather than try another description.

- **Only** says what was left out the same way.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
