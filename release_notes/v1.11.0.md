**A console that keeps everything.** Every build is now written the same way: the time and what was built on the first line, `Note:` in amber for something to know, `Problem:` in red for something that went wrong, AI lines in magenta, and one closing line, such as `Done: 30 tracks queued in Speakers, 62 not in library, 14 s.` Click the small green arrow at the top of the console for its tabs: **All**, **Main Window**, one per Alexa device, and **Log**. Each tab shows its own latest build, so a voice command in the kitchen no longer wipes the build you're reading at the PC. The **Log** keeps the last 50 builds from each source, with the zone, sources used, hits and misses, what the AI Moderator did and any problems, and numbers Non-stop top-ups in their chain (001, 002, 003). Double-click a row to open that build, then Copy, Query or Export it.

**A user manual.** The zip now includes `24bit7_Manual.pdf`: every tab and setting explained, with screenshots, and the Voice Commands and Alexa setup guides folded in. Open it from **Settings > About**.

**Settings > About.** The version you're running, links to GitHub, the README, the release notes and the manual, the voice commands at a glance, and credits for every service 24bit7 uses.

- **Simple / Advanced** in the console strip replaces the Debug tick. Debug lines are always recorded now: Simple hides them, Advanced shows them, and Export to Log always includes them.
- **Failed voice commands show in the console**, under the device's tab and in the Log, with what Alexa said. Before, they went nowhere.
- **Lines that only confirm normal working**, such as fast start or a stopped zone starting, have moved to debug.
- **AI is magenta** throughout the app.
- **New tagline:** Perfect Playlists and Music Discovery.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
