**Save Playlist can copy the tracks to your phone, Search plays a few songs from each of several artists, and Change Zone moves the music to another room.**

Tick **Copy Tracks To** when you save a playlist and, with an Android phone plugged in by USB, the tracks go into a folder named after the playlist with an .m3u8 beside them. Give each phone a name, choose the folder, set a limit in GB if you like, and tick Clear Music First to start from a clean slate. Save the same playlist again and only the new tracks are copied. On the Search tab, click into the Artist box for a list of your own artists, **+ Add** as many as you like, and a green **Go** plays so many songs you own by each, shuffled with the artists spread out.

- **Change Zone**, beside Stop and Skip Track on the Now Playing row, moves what's playing to another zone or to Multiroom, starting this track again there or carrying on from the next track.
- **Most Played** is the new name for Use Adaptive Selections, and Artist's Top Tracks has a tick of its own for it, off to start: ticked, Top 15 is up to fifteen of the artist's widely played songs, so a one-hit wonder gives just the hit.
- Discover keeps a Radio listen as one session, however many rounds it plays in the same zone.
- Deezer now finds bands it spells with different punctuation, such as P.O.D., rather than an empty namesake.
- Search's note moved into the console while the tab is open, and the boxes are centred.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
