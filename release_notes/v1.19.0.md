**Playlists can now go to Tidal.** Pick Tidal under Output on the Play tab and a build fills your Tidal playlist called 24bit7, in place of what was there, ready to play in the Tidal app, on the web or on your phone. When it's done, the console offers Open in Tidal app and Open in Tidal web. Like YouTube, it's for listening beyond your library, so tracks are found on Tidal by name rather than checked against your files. To keep a playlist you like, rename it in Tidal: the next build makes a fresh 24bit7.

To set it up, sign in once under Settings > Keys with **Sign In to Tidal** (the ? beside it has the steps for your Tidal app at developer.tidal.com), then tick Tidal under Settings > Other > Additional Outputs.

- **Tidal on Discover too.** Tick rows and **Create Tidal Playlist** puts them in the same 24bit7 playlist, so you can hear the misses before you buy. A Tidal button searches the selected track there, like the YouTube one. Both are ticked under Settings > Discover.
- **Settings > Discover** is the new name for Settings > Search, as everything on it sets up the Discover tab. Record Label now sits with the reference sites, and a new **Create Playlist** section picks which playlist buttons Discover shows.
- **Additional Outputs** under Settings > Other picks which services the Output list offers. YouTube's settings have moved there, under its own tick. YouTube stays ticked, so nothing changes unless you want it to.
- **Review** gets **Send to Tidal** when Output is Tidal.
- **Better name matching everywhere.** Every mode now treats accents, "$" and "+" the same way: JAŸ-Z matches JAY-Z, Travi$ Scott matches Travis Scott, and Halcyon + On + On matches Halcyon And On And On. Tidal lookups also forgive a title one letter out by the same artist, as library matching already did.
- **Clearer console lines for YouTube and Tidal output:** "On Tidal" and "Not on Tidal" instead of "In library", which didn't apply.
- **Settings > About** names Tidal among the sources and in the credits.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
