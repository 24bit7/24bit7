**Forget on the Devices tab always works, and a phone that isn't answering can be reconnected with its code.**

Forget sometimes didn't stick: a search already under way put the phone back, without its code, so it showed as "not found" with no way to reconnect it. That can't happen now.

- A phone over WiFi that isn't answering has **Type Its Code...** beside Rename and Forget. Type the code on the phone's screen and 24bit7 finds it on your home network and reconnects it, keeping its name, folder and records.
- Forget looks across the network straight away, so a phone whose app is still open is asked about at once rather than five minutes later. Opening the tab looks too, unless it did in the last half minute.
- The tab draws once when it first opens, rather than an empty page and then the folder.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
