**Stack albums and playlists on Playlists & Albums, and save to JRiver, your phone or both with one Save.**

Each row of the Album or Playlist list now has **+ Add**. Add as many albums and playlists as you like, then Play, Embellish or Save takes the lot: Play Ordered plays them back to back, each in its own order, and Play Shuffled mixes them all. Save Playlist and Copy to Phone are now one **Save**, which saves what the last playlist played or, once you've picked or stacked something since, what's in the box, without playing it. Its window has a name and two ticks, **As a JRiver Playlist** and **Copy Tracks To**, so one save can do both.

- Clicking a name still picks that one on its own, as before.
- A stack copied to the phone is one playlist there, named in the Save window.
- Embellish takes a stack as one playlist, with the amounts.
- The console says each one you add or take off, with the count.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
