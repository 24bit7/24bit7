**Debug works in the app, and you can export a log.** Every `[debug]` line and every source's result now reach the console (they went to a stdout that doesn't exist under pythonw before). Click into the console for **Export to Log**: it writes a dated file to `logs\` with your version, Python, Windows and JRiver versions, library size, your settings with every key and password hidden, and the console, then opens the folder. Paste it into a forum post and the whole picture is there.

**AI replies were being cut off.** Similar Tracks and AI Playlist asked Claude for 40 songs with a 2,000-token limit, and Claude Sonnet 5 spends most of that thinking before it writes. A reply cut short couldn't be read, so the source reported 0 tracks and nothing was cached; a later run that happened to fit was cached, which is why AI alone could fail, then work. Now the list calls run with thinking off (cheaper and faster, about a third of the tokens), the limit is 4,000, a reply that still can't be read whole keeps every complete entry, and the console says what happened: `AI: 40 similar tracks`, `(from cache)`, or `(reply cut short, 34 recovered)`.

- **One copy at a time.** Launching 24bit7 while it's already running closes the old copy, so keyboard shortcuts and the tray icon always belong to the one you can see.
- **"AI request sent, cogitating..."** while a request is out, with the time taken at Debug level.
- **Similar Tracks summary line:** `Suggested 40, checked 38, in library 24.`
- **Shortcut refusals** show the Windows error code rather than assuming another program has the key.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
