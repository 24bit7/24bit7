**The console's Copy, Clear, Query, Export to Log and Simple buttons sit at the top of the console, level with its arrow.**

They used to sit on the console's first line, where they looked out of place and covered the end of a long line. Now they appear in the strip above the text when you click into the console, as they do in the tab row when the console's tabs are open.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
