**Songs you own in more than one format now get past your filters, and "only ACDC" finds AC/DC.** If you keep a song as a stereo MP3 and a surround FLAC, 24bit7 used to pick one copy and drop the song if your filters didn't allow that copy, even when the other one would have passed. It now tries your other copies first. And Only, by voice or typed, now finds an artist whatever Alexa does with the gaps and punctuation in the name.

- **Filters:** where a filter or the hidden-track check leaves out one copy of a song, your other copies are tried, and the first that passes is used. A live recording doesn't count as a copy of the studio one.
- **Recently played** now counts any copy, so playing the MP3 yesterday keeps the FLAC out today too.
- **Only** finds an artist with or without the gaps in its name: "ACDC" finds AC/DC, "gusgus" finds Gus Gus.
- **Names with N:** "Guns and Roses" finds Guns N' Roses, and "Salt and Pepa" finds Salt-N-Pepa. This works everywhere 24bit7 matches names, not just Only.
- **Apostrophes:** a straight apostrophe and a curly one now count as the same, so an artist tagged both ways (Jane's Addiction, say) is one artist, not two.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
