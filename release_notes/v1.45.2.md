**A phone you add shows on the Devices tab straight away, and the first sort of an older phone says how far it's got.**

Adding a second phone could look as if it hadn't worked: the tab kept showing only the first phone while it sorted the new one's old folder over WiFi, which can take half an hour, without saying anything.

- A phone shows at the top of the tab as soon as it's added, while its folder is still being read.
- The one-off sort of a folder copied to by a 24bit7 before 1.44 counts the files as they move ("31 of 84 files moved"). Keep the 24bit7 app open on the phone until it has finished.
- A phone that can't be read no longer hides the others.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
