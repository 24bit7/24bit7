**Switch zones by voice.** "Alexa, ask needle drop to switch" moves what's playing to another room, at the same song and the same point in it, and the room it left stops. With three or more rooms, Alexa asks which, or say "switch to the kitchen". Pick the zones under Settings > Voice Commands > Enable Switch To. Switching to a Sonos or other DLNA zone starts the song from the beginning.

**Stop, pause and resume by voice**, on the speaker's own zone, so you don't need the TV on to stop the music in the living room.

**Keep it going.** "Alexa, ask needle drop to keep it going" turns on Non-stop for anything playing, even an album you started in JRiver.

**Blend for JRiver playlists.** Settings > JRiver Playlists > Blend weaves new music into a playlist, one of yours then one new, from similar artists or similar tracks, up to 50 new songs.

- Alexa no longer reads every command when it doesn't understand. Say "list commands" to hear them.
- New keyboard shortcuts: Switch Zones and Keep It Going.
- Long album closers are skipped while a playlist is built, so it reaches its full length, and the Done line counts what was actually sent.
- A test suite in tests\, run with py -m pytest tests.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across. Then paste the updated skill files (alexa\) into the Alexa developer console.
