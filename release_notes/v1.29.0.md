**Playlists you own, nested behind two buttons: the Play tab starts with three.**

The Play buttons are now playlists you own, and a playlist can sit under another. Out of the box the row is **Similar Artists**, **Artist's Top Tracks** and **AI Playlist**, and the first two have a small arrow that opens a menu of more. Under Similar Artists: **Track Matching** (what Similar Tracks did), **Inner Circle** (the seed's three closest artists, then only the artists their own lists agree on: tight, and sometimes short, which it says) and **Headliner** (an artist's top tracks, two at a time, with one track from a close similar artist between them). Artist's Top Tracks plays fifteen in random order, with **Top 10, Best First** and **Top 10, Best Last** in its menu. Press the button to build, or pick a row from the menu; right-click a row to make it the button's default, and the button carries its name.

- **Settings > Playlists** lists every playlist with a tick each (ticked means it's on the Play tab, in list order), nested ones indented under their parent. Click a name to rename it; the six buttons beside the list add, delete, move, nest and bring out; a parent's page has an **Alternate Title** for how its own row reads in its menu. Each playlist is a whole recipe: one or more configs (Artist Matching, Track Matching, Artist's Top Tracks or AI Playlist, with that kind's sources, Must agree, Moderator and counts), then Drift, Deep Cuts, Non-stop, the filters it runs under and a shortcut. **+ Add Another Config** puts a second way of finding tracks under the first, and the **Mix** section says how they come together: Shuffle, Append, or Alternate by a pattern. Headliner is built that way, and you can build the same yourself.
- **Drag.** Hold a button to drag it along the row, the others moving aside as you go; hold it over another button for a moment and it nests there; drop it on the **Settings** or **Remove** pill that appears over the console. Rows drag out of a menu the same way.
- **Add a Playlist**, from + on the Play tab: **From Existing** adds one back or makes a copy, **From Template** puts one of the four kinds as shipped, Headliner, Inner Circle or a Top 10 on the row at once, and **New** is a blank slate whose page opens on the name box.
- **Inner Circle** is a mode on any Artist Matching or Track Matching config: the circle's size (three to five) and how many of their lists must agree. With Seed on Playing Now, the sampled artists or tracks are the circle.
- **More Options** keeps Seed, Play Mode, Show Credits, + Add JRiver Playlist and ?. A playlist's Moderator, Drift, Deep Cuts and Non-stop are on its own page; dragging it onto the Settings pill takes you there.
- **Settings > Devices** replaces Zones: each Alexa device has a page with its name, the zone it plays to, Enable Switch To and the playlist each phrase runs (unset, the standard one of that kind). Show, Default and Follow are back under Settings > Other > Zones, and Test My Sources with its score tables is under Other too.
- **Coming from 1.28:** your settings become the standard playlists, arranged as above, with your filters on them. One change is made for you: Artist's Top Tracks is set to fifteen tracks in random order; everything else about it keeps your settings, and its page puts the count and order back if you prefer. Non-stop follows the playlist that built the music, shortcuts are set on each playlist's page, and Profiles carry your playlists and devices.
- **Output** has moved to the right of the Zone line, and **AI usage**, if you show it, to the row with the Now Playing and Search tabs.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
