**Headliner: a new Play button that gives one artist the stage, with the closest similar artists as guests.**

Press Headliner and you get the artist's top tracks, as Artist's Top Tracks plays them, but every third track is a guest spot. The guests are the closest similar artists in your library, one track each. Each guest's track is the one your sources most often play next to the headliner's own hits, so the console might say "played alongside In the Waiting Line"; failing that, it's their most popular track you own. If fewer guests turn up than there are spots, the console says so and the headliner fills the rest. It uses Artist's Top Tracks' settings and never spends AI credits. From the Search tab it needs only the artist.

- **Output** has moved to the right of the Zone line, on both the Now Playing and Search tabs, to make room for the new button.
- **AI usage**, if you show it in Now Playing, now sits on the row with the Now Playing and Search tabs, so it shows on Search too.
- **More Options:** switches that are Off, and ones you can't click, are a lighter grey, so the ones that are on stand out.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
