**Playlists you own: tick what's on the Play tab, build your own from configs, and two new ready-made ones.**

The Play buttons are now playlists you own. Each one is a whole recipe (a way of finding tracks with its sources and settings, the filters it runs under, a shortcut), and **Settings > Playlists** lists them with a tick each: ticked means it's on the Play tab, in list order. Click a name to rename it, Move Up and Move Down reorder, and **+** on the Play tab offers the unticked ones and **New...**. Hold a button to drag it along the row, or onto the cog (its settings) or the cross (off the row) that appear over the console. **More Options** now shows the Moderator, Drift, Deep Cuts and Non-stop of the playlist you last used, live: change one and it sticks to that playlist; with the bar open, click another playlist to see its settings, and click it again to build. Two playlists arrive ready-made: **Headliner** (an artist's top tracks, two at a time, with one track from a close similar artist between them) and **Inner Circle** (the seed's three closest artists, then only the artists their own lists agree on: tight, and sometimes short, which it says). Everything you had carries over: the four standard playlists start with today's settings and nothing about them changes.

- **Configs and Mix.** A playlist's page is one scrolling page: its config (Artist Matching, Track Matching, Artist's Top Tracks or AI Playlist, with that kind's sources, Must agree, Moderator and counts), then the settings for the whole playlist. **+ Add Another Config** puts a second way of finding tracks under the first, and the **Mix** section that appears says how they come together: Shuffle, Append, or Alternate by a pattern, plus which config Drift carries on from and which Non-stop carries on as. Headliner is Artist's Top Tracks and Artist Matching alternating two to one, and you can build the same yourself.
- **Inner Circle** is a mode on any Artist Matching or Track Matching config: the circle's size (three to five) and how many of their lists must agree. With Seed on Playing Now, the sampled artists or tracks are the circle.
- **New Playlist** asks one thing: what it should be like. A template (the four kinds as shipped, Headliner or Inner Circle) or one of your playlists; the name is filled in and the page opens.
- **Settings > Devices** replaces Zones: each Alexa device has a page with its name, the zone it plays to, Enable Switch To and the playlist each phrase runs (unset, the standard one of that kind). Show, Default and Follow are back under Settings > Other > Zones, and Test My Sources with its score tables is under Other too.
- **+ Add JRiver Playlist** is the options bar's name for adding one of your JRiver playlists, now that 24bit7's own are playlists too.
- **Non-stop follows the playlist** that built the music, all evening, and shortcuts are set on each playlist's page. **Profiles** carry your playlists and devices.
- **Output** has moved to the right of the Zone line, and **AI usage**, if you show it, to the row with the Now Playing and Search tabs.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
