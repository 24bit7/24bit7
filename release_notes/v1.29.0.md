**Your own Play buttons, and zones with settings of their own.**

The Play buttons are now a list you own. Each one is a whole recipe: a kind of build, its sources and playlist settings, the filters it runs under and a keyboard shortcut. Press **+** to add a button, right-click one to rename, move or remove it, and **Headliner** arrives ready-made: an artist's top tracks with every third track a guest spot from the closest similar artists you own. **Settings > Buttons** replaces Sources, Playlist and Filters, with one page per button. **Settings > Zones** gives each JRiver zone a page: whether it shows on the Play tab and is the default, which button each voice phrase runs there, the filters every playlist sent to it must pass, and whether Non-stop may top playlists up. An Alexa device keeps only its name and its zone. Everything you had carries over: the four buttons start with today's settings, a device that had settings of its own becomes a button its zone points to, and your filters are ticked where they used to apply.

- **Non-stop follows the button** that built the playlist, all evening, and a zone's Non-stop tick turns top-ups off for everything played there, Keep It Going included.
- **Shortcuts** are set on each button's page. Shuffle Songs by Artist, Switch Zones, Switch Profiles, Keep It Going and the profile shortcuts stay under Settings > Other.
- **Profiles** carry your buttons and zones. A profile saved before this version loads into the standard buttons.
- **Output** has moved to the right of the Zone line, on both the Now Playing and Search tabs, to make room for the buttons. **AI usage**, if you show it, now sits on the row with the Now Playing and Search tabs.
- **More Options** keeps Seed, Mode, Show Credits, + Add Playlist and the ? for hover notes; Moderator, Drift and Deep Cuts live on each button's pages. Switches that are Off, and ones you can't click, are a lighter grey.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
