**Dragging the window no longer crashes 24bit7.** With the built-in title bar, dragging an empty part of the tab strip closed the app. Moving, snapping and double-click to maximise all work again.

**The AI Moderator removes the track it names.** It could remove the song next to the one its reason described. It now checks the number, artist and title agree before removing anything, and keeps the track if they don't.

**The AI Moderator copes with a note after its answer.** An extra line from the AI could make the check fail with "Extra data". The answer is now read on its own and anything around it is ignored.

If a song seems to go missing without a "Removed" line, it was most likely trimmed: 24bit7 finds a few extra tracks when the moderator is on, then cuts back to your playlist length.

Thanks to mandrews79 for the reports and logs on GitHub.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one. The Alexa skill hasn't changed this time.
