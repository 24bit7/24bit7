**Search now asks "Did you mean...?" when you mistype an artist you own.** Type "eleven finger" and the build ends with "Did you mean Finger Eleven?": click it and 24bit7 builds again from the right artist. It catches words in the wrong order and small typos, only ever suggests artists in your library, and never uses a name until you click.

- **The manual** has a short tip on crossfading in JRiver: S-Curve at about six seconds, with gapless kept for albums played in order.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
