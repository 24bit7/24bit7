**A Playlist Settings page that starts simple, More Options switches that override every playlist, and rounded corners throughout.**

Settings > Playlists and Settings > Devices become one tab, **Playlist Settings**, redrawn so it doesn't put new listeners off. **Simple** shows each playlist as one config with its Sources, Playlist Selection and Filters, and nothing else runs; **Advanced** shows every section, open when it's on and closed when it's off, with a two-sentence note on each header and a **Take a Tour** that walks them one at a time on Artist + Guests. The Play tab's **More Options** row gets its Moderator, Deep Cuts and Non-stop switches back: while the row is open they override every playlist's own settings, and closed, the pages decide.

- **Your Playlist Buttons** lists what's on the Play tab, with no ticks; **Device Playlists** below it has a tab per Alexa device, its zone and Switch To, and the playlist each phrase runs, greyed as Standard until you give the device its own.
- **Each config is a box**: sources across in one row, Must agree under them, Playlist Selection as sentences with the numbers in them, and Filters of its own at the end.
- **Name**, **Title in Menu** and a **Preview** of the button with its menu sit at the top of every playlist's page.
- **Consensus** can finish a short playlist with plain matching: tick If Consensus finds fewer than, set the number, and the console says how many came from each.
- **Inner Circle is now Consensus and Headliner is Artist + Guests.** A playlist still carrying the old shipped name is renamed; one you renamed yourself is left alone.
- **Seed on Playing Now** colours the buttons and menu rows that follow it, in teal, or leaf green if your accent is teal.
- **Rounded corners** on every box and number box in Settings.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
