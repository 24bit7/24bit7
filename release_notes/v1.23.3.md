**Non-stop no longer starts the music again after you've pressed Stop.** If you stopped or paused the last track while Non-stop was finding more, JRiver started playing again a few seconds later when the new tracks arrived. Now, when you've stopped or paused it, nothing is added and the console says so. Press play on that last track and Non-stop carries on as before. When the last track simply runs out before the new tracks are ready, they're added and play as usual.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
