**24bit7 no longer moves anything that's already on your phone. What it didn't put there shows on the Devices tab under Other Files.**

Until now, the first time 24bit7 used a phone's folder, it sorted everything already there into Albums. That included the phone owner's own music and other apps' folders. Now 24bit7 only ever copies into Albums and Playlists. Anything else stays exactly where it is.

- Other Files sits under Albums and Playlists on the Devices tab. Its folders open, and you can tick and delete things there just as you can in Albums. With nothing else in the folder, it doesn't show.
- Clear Playlists First still empties Playlists only.
- The first time the Devices tab shows a phone's folder, a short note explains how 24bit7 uses it. It comes once.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
