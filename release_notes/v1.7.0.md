**Your own playlists join in.** Add any JRiver playlist or smartlist to a build from the Play tab: before 24bit7's picks, after them, or mixed through, spaced evenly or at random.

- **Added playlists.** Open More options, press Add playlist, pick a playlist and choose Add before, Add after or Mix. Rows play in the order listed and can be dragged to reorder. They come through as JRiver gives them, with only songs already in the playlist dropped. Play tab only, with JRiver output.
- **More options.** One row for Show Credits, the AI Moderator and Add playlist. It remembers whether it's open, and counts what's on when it's closed.
- **AI Moderator on the Play tab.** Switch it on or off as you go, for Similar Artists and Similar Tracks. Voice devices with settings of their own keep theirs.
- **Run after building.** Settings > Playlist can run a .bat, .exe or script once a playlist is in JRiver, per Play option and per device.
- **Purple for AI.** Anything that spends Anthropic credit is marked in purple.
- **Tidier header.** The tagline sits beside the logo, and the track title is smaller.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
