**Deep Cuts: playlists can now reach the corners of your collection you rarely play.** Switch on Deep Cuts in the Play tab's More Options and each playlist is topped up with tracks from the seed's genre that rarely get a turn. Similar Artists picks the artists you've played least in the last year, then their best known tracks; Similar Tracks and AI Playlist pick the tracks you've played least. They're added on top of the playlist, by 10% to start (three more on a 30-track playlist), either Spaced evenly through it or Shuffled in. The genre comes from your own Genre tags, so it works best when they're well organised. Each build's console says what was added, or why nothing was.

- **How many:** Settings > Playlist > Deep Cuts sets the top-up for Similar Artists, Similar Tracks and AI Playlist, from 10% to 100% (an even mix). Artist's Top Tracks doesn't use it.
- **Checked like everything else:** your Filters and Skip Played apply, very short tracks and hidden closers are left out, there's never more than one per artist, and the Moderator checks them too. With YouTube or Tidal output, they're looked up there.
- **Moderator:** the AI Moderator is now called the Moderator. It's still magenta while it's on, as it uses the AI, and your usage history carries across.
- **Non-stop** shows as a bigger, bold **∞** on the Play tab. Settings and the console still call it Non-stop.
- **Hover notes on the Play tab are now off until you want them.** Click the **?** at the right of the More Options row and every control explains itself when you rest the pointer on it. Notes saying why something is greyed out always show.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
