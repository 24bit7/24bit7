**Radio plays what you listen to with no seed, your own JRiver playlists can be played or embellished from the Play tab, and Top-Up adds the next playlist to what's already queued.**

**Radio** sorts the artists you play into stations by the genres the sources give them, such as Classic Rock or Downtempo. Each station has pillar artists: your own most played first, then the genre's best that you own, and Edit on a station's row lets you remove any or add any artist in your library. Each round is Consensus over a few pillars, about an hour long, and it carries on from the pillars all evening; an artist that belongs in another station is left out. It's a playlist like the others, so it can be moved, deleted or added again from a template. Its page sets how many stations there are, lets you rename them, and holds the sources, filters, the Moderator and Deep Cuts, one set for every Radio button. It plays the last station you played, and its arrow lists every station to split, join back or add as a button of its own. A new **JRiver Playlists** tab plays any of your playlists or smartlists, shuffled or in order, or embellishes it with the top tracks of artists the playlist's own artists agree on.

- **Play & Embellish** adds 10%, 20% or 50% more, doubles or triples the playlist, or builds it out with every agreeing artist, and says plainly when there's little to add. Settings > JRiver Playlists picks which of those the arrow offers and which the button runs.
- **Save Playlist** appears after a build to a JRiver zone and saves that zone's Playing Now as a JRiver playlist, so a chain of Top-Ups or an evening of Radio can be kept.
- **Top-Up**, on the More Options row: click it, then a playlist, and the new tracks are shuffled into what's left of Playing Now instead of replacing it. It lasts one playlist.
- **Match Tempo** for Artist Matching and Track Matching: Off, Prefer or Strict, from your BPM tags, with half and double time counting as a match.
- **Radio and Top-Up by voice**: "Alexa, ask needle drop for radio folk", or just "radio" to hear your last five stations and pick one; "top up", then any command, adds to what's playing instead of replacing it. The skill needs its new interaction model and code pasted in, as the manual's Upgrading section says.
- **Voice skips are counted** in a JRiver field of 24bit7's own. The new **Skipped Tracks** filter, off to start, leaves out a track you've skipped twice and an artist with three such tracks, both numbers yours to set, and lists what it leaves out, each with Let Back In.
- **Every playlist**: any filter can now apply to every playlist, ticked on its page or not.
- **At least**: a filter can ask for a share of each playlist instead of leaving tracks out, such as at least 20% New Zealand music, and its rules can read any JRiver field by name, your own included. Short of the share, the usual tracks fill up and the console says by how much.
- **Console Query** reads each playlist's own settings and the manual, not the old code, so its suggestions name the right control, playlist and config, and say what to change it to. It now uses your AI's main model, as AI Playlist does, which keeps to them better than the quick one, for a few pence more.
- **Settings > Devices** is a tab of its own again, so Playlist Settings is less busy.
- Non-stop now "adds more" rather than "tops up", leaving the word to Top-Up, and the German uses JRiver's own names for Playing Now and Media Network.

Radio needs some play history: it rests, greyed, until there are forty artists you've played ten or more times.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
