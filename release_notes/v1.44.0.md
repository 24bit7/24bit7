**A new Devices tab shows what's on your phone, albums and playlists side by side, and Select From Hits picks an artist's songs at random from their hits.**

The Devices tab appears after Settings once 24bit7 knows a phone or a folder. Each device has a green or grey dot for whether it's answering, with Rename, Forget, and how much is on it against your limit. On the phone, Save now copies a playlist into Music/Playlists, each song named "Artist - Title", and albums you add on the Devices tab go into Music/Albums, so Clear Playlists First never touches your albums. The first time a phone is used, what older copies left is sorted into the two, and the console says what moved.

- **Albums and Playlists** sit side by side on the tab. Tick and Delete, with the amber line saying what goes first. A song can be taken out of one playlist on its own.
- **Add to the phone from your library**: type an artist, album or track over Albums and click + Add.
- **Select From Hits** replaces Most Played: Off, ↓ (fewer songs for an artist with fewer hits) or ↕ (also up to half as many again for one with plenty). The songs are picked at random from the hits, so each build is different.
- **+ Add a Phone Over WiFi looks twice**, more slowly the second time, before saying no phone answered.
- Manage Folder is now the Devices tab; Save's link reads Open Devices.
- A wrong key in the Alexa skill is explained, rather than heard as 24bit7 not answering. Copying one track says "1 track".

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
