**Artists with dotted initials are found in your library again.** When a source spelled an act without its dots (UNKLE, REM, MIA) and your tags kept them (U.N.K.L.E., R.E.M., M.I.A.), Similar Tracks reported the tracks as not in your library, even though you own them.

- Similar Tracks, Test My Sources and voice requests all match these artists again, whichever way either side spells them.
- Test My Sources scores may rise slightly if your library has dotted acts. Re-run it to refresh your results.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
