**Tidal playlists now open straight away, in the Tidal app.** Once a build, Review's Send to Tidal or Discover's Create Tidal Playlist has filled your 24bit7 playlist, it opens in the Tidal app, ready to play. If you'd rather use Tidal's website, choose **Tidal Web** under Settings > Other > Additional Outputs > Tidal > **Open In**. Without the Tidal app installed, it opens on the website and says so. The console still offers the other one, in case you want it that time.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
