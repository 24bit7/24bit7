**The dark theme's green is softer, and you can choose your own accent colour.**

The old green glared on many screens, laptops especially, so the dark theme now uses a gentler one. To pick another, go to Settings > Other > Accent Colour. Choose Colour opens Windows' colour picker, and a preview underneath shows the logo, two buttons and the console in your colour before you restart. The colour covers the dark theme's tabs, buttons and titles, and the console in either theme. AI buttons stay magenta, so you can always tell what uses AI credits.

- A colour too dark to read is made lighter, and one close to magenta is moved away from it.
- The "bit" in the logo takes the opposite colour to your accent.
- The accent is saved in Profiles.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
