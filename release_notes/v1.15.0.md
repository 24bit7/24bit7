**Review, in 24bit7.** Set Behaviour to Review (under More Options, for every build from the Play tab) and the tracks are listed in the console area instead of being sent to JRiver. Tick them in the order you want, then Add as Up Next, Add to End, Finish This Song, Load as New, Stop Song, Play Now, or Save as Playlist. The actions go wherever Output says; with YouTube they become Play on YouTube. Preview in plays a track in another zone (headphones, a Sonos) before you add it, and Settings > Playlist has a Review column so a Review build can aim for a shortlist while Play keeps the full playlist.

- **The AI Playlist window.** Create a playlist from a theme with idea chips, or Steer what's playing in a direction: a seed (the current track or all of Playing Now), an optional tone with Assess Tone, direction chips that combine (Dancier, Faster, Darker, Older...), your own words, and A Little or A Lot. Faster and Slower are checked against BPM tags. Both modes have Tracks and If Short (Ask Again, Drift or Leave Short), and everything is remembered.
- **Profiles** in Settings > Other: save your settings as DJ, Explore, No AI and so on, and Load switches between them.
- **Use AI** under the Anthropic key: Off pauses every AI feature without removing the key.
- **Notes on titles**: the ? marks are gone; rest the pointer on a setting's title for its note.
- **Polish**: the top strip runs to the very top of the window, Settings > Keys shows AI Usage and the Guide as proper tables, and the Review list follows the theme.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one. Profiles live in a `profiles` folder beside 24bit7; copy that too if you've made any.
