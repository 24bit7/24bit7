**Tidal joins the sources, and you can now test which sources suit your library.** Tidal is now a source for Similar Tracks, Similar Artists and Artist's Top Tracks, so someone with only Tidal credentials still gets complete builds. Its Similar Artists results are weaker than its tracks, so it's marked "!!" there, with a note explaining why. To help you choose between sources, each Sources section in Settings now has a **Test My Sources** button. It runs a set of seed tracks across 17 genres against your own library in the background, then shows each source's Score, Best At and Worst At beside it, with Full Results in a pop-up. Until you run your own test, the figures shown come from my library, with a note saying yours will differ.

- **Drift rescue.** With Drift on Custom Sources, a first round that finds nothing no longer stops Drift. It starts again from the original track with Drift's own sources and carries on (found by datdude).
- **If All Else Fails, your way.** When a build finds nothing, you can now choose Shuffle Genre, Run File or Play Playlist. It's off by default, and Shuffle Genre is the default choice once switched on.
- **A second try from the AI.** When none of the AI's suggestions are in your library, 24bit7 asks once more for different ones. This applies to Similar Artists, Similar Tracks and Drift's AI rounds.
- **Better seed matching.** Joint credits fall back to the lead artist, ListenBrainz gets a MusicBrainz route for tracks it can't find directly, and Tidal tries title variants.
- **Drift's sources note** in Settings > Playlist now explains the trade-off between same and custom sources, rather than telling you which to pick.
- **Support:** alongside PayPal for AI credits, there's now a Ko-fi fund towards a pair of PMC Prodigy speakers.
- **Fixes:** AI replies that came back with extra words around the list were wrongly reported as "cut short", and are now read properly with one retry. YouTube no longer says it "needs a track to seed from" in Playing Now and Drift (both found by Denis). On a new install, the first time sources were asked at once could hit a database error and quietly lose one source's answer.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
