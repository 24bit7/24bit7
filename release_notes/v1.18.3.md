**The AI Moderator gives steadier answers, and Drift explains what it drops.** Run the same seed twice and the Moderator could remove different tracks each time, as AI replies vary a little on every request (reported by mandrews79). Its checks now ask for the most consistent answer each provider allows, and Strict keeps a track that's only borderline rather than tossing a coin on it.

- **Drift's chain drop names what it drops.** When the Moderator flags half or more of what one seed brought in, the rest of that batch goes too. The console now names those tracks, rather than saying "the other 1 went too", and says "half or more" rather than "most" (reported by mandrews79).
- **A chain drop never empties a round.** If dropping a chain would leave a Drift round with nothing, the Moderator's own removals stand and the rest of the batch stays, with a console line saying so. Before, one seed's batch could be thrown out whole and end the build on a single track.
- If a provider won't accept the steadier setting, 24bit7 asks again without it and says so once in the console.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env, 24bit7.db and profiles folder across from the old one.
