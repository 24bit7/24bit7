**Review before you play.** The Search tab has a new Behaviour setting: Play Instantly, or Review Mode, which loads the playlist into JRiver's Playing Now and leaves it stopped on track one. Remove tracks, change the order or add them to another playlist in JRiver, then press play. Building from Now Playing, voice commands, shortcuts and Non-stop still play straight away.

- **Variety is now optional for Similar Tracks**, and off by default: you get the closest matches, in order. Turn it on in Settings > Playlist or on the Play tab under More Options. It's set per device, so voice can keep it while the app doesn't.
- **Limit total tracks** for Similar Artists: untick it to keep every track found. With Tracks per artist selection the same as the top tracks (say 5 of 5), there's no random pick at all.
- **Cleaner boxes and buttons** throughout Settings, Discover and AI Playlist: thin flat borders instead of 3D edges.

Thanks to datdude on the JRiver forum for the ideas behind all three.

**Upgrading:** unzip to a new folder and copy your .env and 24bit7.db across from the old one.
