Multiroom: Speakers stopped (Sonos stopped).

Notes

  • Stop, amber text and border: stops the Now Playing zone in JRiver. In multiroom both rooms stop, since the Speakers follow the Sonos.
  • Once the zone has stopped the button reads Clear Playing Now in red. One click empties the list, no confirmation (Ben, 9 Nov 2026). It turns back into Stop as soon as the zone plays.
  • Skip Track: the next track in the zone's Playing Now; a fresh start, so the two rooms stay in step. Greyed while the zone is stopped or nothing follows the current track.
  • Both work for any zone, multiroom or not. By voice, needle drop's stop and next do the same thing.
  • Hover notes (Play tab style, shown with the ? on): "Stop: stops this zone. In multiroom, both rooms." "Clear Playing Now: empties this zone's list." "Skip Track: the next track in this zone's list."
